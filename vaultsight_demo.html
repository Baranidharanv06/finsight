Who may load new filings?
Guest and employee should be refused. Manager and executive really re-ingest the filing, which takes about a minute.
1. Identity
Amazon Cognito signs the user in. The role comes from the user's group in the signed token, never from the request.
2. Decision
Each retrieved chunk is scored from its relevance and the gap between its sensitivity and the user's clearance. Cleared: full text. Above clearance: summary or redacted, never full.
3. Evidence
The decision for every chunk is written to DynamoDB before the answer is generated. The request id shown on each card is that audit record.
